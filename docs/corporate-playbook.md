# Corporate Playbook — Village Translations

Every corporate ritual Kenny knows, translated to village form.
The rule: he sees routine, they see divinity. Play it straight from both sides.

---

## Daily / Weekly

**Standup** → Morning gathering. Each villager reports what they gathered yesterday, what they'll gather today, and any blockers ("the north bush is bare").
They see: daily audience with the god. He sees: standup.

**Meetings about having too many meetings** → The god keeps gathering everyone to discuss whether they're gathering too often. The villagers are confused but honored.
They see: a mysterious deep ritual. He sees: meta meeting hell.

**Weekly planning** → Harvest planning. Which bushes, which villagers, targets for the week.
They see: the god divines the week's providence. He sees: sprint planning.

## Bi-weekly

**Sprint planning** → Planting and harvest cycle planning. Bigger bets: "this fortnight we focus on berries to stockpile for feast day."
They see: the god lays out the great plan. He sees: sprint planning.

**Sprint retro** → After feast day: what went well, what didn't, one thing to try. Villagers speak honestly because the god asked.
They see: the god reflects on the harvest and seeks wisdom. He sees: retro.

## 1:1s and Check-ins

**1 on 1s** → The god speaks to a single villager privately. Career growth, concerns, "how are you feeling about the bush assignment?"
They see: a personal blessing, a private audience. The highest honor. He sees: 1:1.

**Manager check-in** → The god checks on the most senior villager (the de facto team lead). "How's the team doing?"
They see: the god consults his high priest. He sees: manager sync.

**Skip level check-in** → The god talks to a junior villager directly, bypassing the senior. The senior villager is quietly unsettled.
They see: the god's attention is unpredictable and vast. He sees: skip level.

**Department head check-ins** → The god meets the best forager, the best cook, the best builder separately.
They see: the god honors each craft. He sees: stakeholder syncs.

**Director check-ins** → The god meets with the village elders. Slow, political, nothing decided.
They see: the god communes with the ancient ones. He sees: director sync.

**Quarterly CEO check-in** → Once a season, the god goes quiet for a day. Sits alone, stares at the horizon. Villagers leave offerings and whisper.
They see: the god in deep contemplation, communing with higher powers. He sees: staring at the wall questioning his life choices.

## All-Hands

**Company all hands** → Feast day speech. Everyone gathers, the god speaks about the quarter's harvest, the vision, the road ahead.
They see: divine address. He sees: all-hands.

**Department all hands** → All foragers gather. Or all cooks. Smaller, craft-specific.
They see: the god blesses each trade. He sees: department all-hands.

## Design

**Design meeting** → Planning a new building. Sketching in dirt, debating placement.
They see: the god dreams new structures into being. He sees: design review.

**Design alignment meetings** → Arguing about where the kitchen goes. Again. For the third time.
They see: divine deliberation, the god weighs many futures. He sees: alignment hell.

---

## How to use this doc

When designing a feature, check if a ritual maps to it. The ritual gives you:
1. The villager-facing flavor (what they call it, how they react)
2. The manager-facing UI copy (deadpan corporate language)
3. A free joke that writes itself

Never explain the joke. Both sides are serious.
