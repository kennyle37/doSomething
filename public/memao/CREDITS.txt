Memao Fantasy Character Sprite Pack
by Sleeping Robot Games
https://sleeping-robot-games.itch.io/fantasy-character-sprite-pack

Free pixel art RPG character pack. 10 unique fantasy characters,
originally with idle, walk, run, farming, and emote animations
in 4 directions.

This split extracts per-character animation strips (48x48 cells,
4 frames each) for game use:
  idle_down/up/left/right, walk_down/up/left/right,
  work_down/up/left/right (axe chop), emote_wave, emote_cheer

Check the original pack's license for attribution requirements.
