Serene Village Revamped - Split Assets

You must give appropriate credit to LimeZu when using these assets,
provide a link to the license, and indicate if changes were made.

What was done here:
- The original tileset sheets were split into individual sprite files
  (houses, trees, bushes, rocks, objects, terrain tiles, pickups).
- Split from the 48x48 sheet (highest resolution in the pack).
- Filenames describe the content (e.g. house_red_01.png).
- terrain/fragments/ contains autotile edge pieces from the original
  sheet, kept for completeness.
- tilesets/terrain_block_48x48.png is the original terrain section
  intact, for future autotile use.
- animated/ contains the original animation strips (water, door, campfire).
